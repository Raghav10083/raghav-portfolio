It's Me Pico

It's Me Pico is a competitive territory-control mobile game designed around quick matches, strategic decision-making, and fast-paced gameplay. Players battle to capture and defend territory before the timer expires, creating a simple yet engaging competitive experience.

  • Game Design
  • Product Planning
  • Publishing
  • AdMob Integration
  • Monetization Strategy
  • User Experience Design
  • Testing & Iteration
  • Mobile Game Publishing: Navigated App Store / Google Play submission loops, optimization and deployment policies.
  • Player Retention: Designed active rewards to retain players during quick match sessions.
  • Monetization Systems: Implemented non-intrusive AdMob flows balancing UX and impressions.
  • Gameplay Balancing: Fine-tuned power-ups and territory capture speed curves for high match intensity.
Concept & Balance: Iterative paper testing of territory-control flow.
AdMob & SDKs: Implementing telemetry, ads, and monetization triggers.
