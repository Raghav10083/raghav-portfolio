It's Me Pico

It's Me Pico is a competitive territory-control mobile game where players battle to capture and defend territory before the timer expires, creating a fast-paced, high-intensity match experience.

  • Game Design
  • Publishing
  • Product Planning
  • AdMob Integration
  • Monetization Strategy
  • User Experience Design
  • Balancing Fast-Paced Matches: Tuning territory capture speed and cooldown metrics so matches felt competitive yet fair.
  • Ad Integration UX: Implementing AdMob rewards and interstitials without breaking the player's competitive game loop.
  • Network Telemetry: Resolving intermittent data drops when logging metrics under low-bandwidth mobile connections.
  • Retention over Monetization: Early retention statistics proved that player experience is a stronger driver of long-term revenue than heavy ad placements.
  • Iterative Beta Testing: Incorporating direct telemetry insights from closed-alpha tests allowed us to re-tune matchmaking criteria.
  • A/B Store Testing: Modifying graphic icons and promotional text keywords boosted organic Google Play page conversions.
Phase 1 // Prototyping: Visualizing hex capture physics and core territory algorithms.
Phase 2 // Integration: Injecting SDK utilities, analytics, and AdMob banner/reward loops.
Phase 3 // Release & Ops: Published on Google Play. Iteratively reviewing player metrics to release patches.

The Legend of Indra vs Vritrasur

A mythology-inspired game project based on the legendary battle between Indra and Vritrasur. The project combines atmospheric acoustic storytelling, thematic soundtracks, and stylized vector environment design assets.

Music Production

  • Theme creation setting epic tone
  • Atmospheric storm weather soundscapes
  • Battle music production & drums scoring
  • Emotional storytelling through sound

Asset Design

  • Asset creation (glowing Vajra, items)
  • Environment support assets mapping
  • Artistic consistency checks
  • Visual development pipelines
  • Atmospheric Scoring: Mixed synthesizers with traditional orchestral drums to evoke the feeling of Indra's lightning and thunder.
  • Stylized Concept Design: Designed environment elements to maintain artistic alignment, ensuring asset textures blended smoothly under variable project lightning.
  • Audio-Visual Synchronization: Modeled environmental audio triggers so visual lightning flashes matched peak musical intensities.
